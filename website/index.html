Every app, one keystroke away.

Cove is a full-screen grid and search for your Mac. Press the hotkey, type a few letters, and press return. Your apps and files stay on your Mac.

Search

Search finds apps and files together.

Type once and the grid filters as you go. The best match gets a highlight, and matching files appear underneath. Search uses Spotlight, so anything Spotlight can find, Cove can find.

      Motion

      Springs with fixed rules.

      Every animation runs on one of seven springs, each with a set duration and bounce. Things that open bounce a little. Things that close never do. With Reduce Motion on, everything becomes a short fade.

      • Open: 0.46 s, bounce 0.24
      • Press: 0.26 s, bounce 0.42
      • Dismiss: 0.26 s, no bounce

      Privacy

      Your data stays on your Mac.

      Cove has no accounts and no analytics. It doesn't send your files, your searches, or your launch history anywhere. The one connection it makes on its own is a daily update check to GitHub, and you can turn that off.

      One update check

      Cove checks GitHub about once a day for new versions. You can turn that off. It never sends your files, searches, or app list.

      No accounts

      There is no sign-in, no profile, and no sync between machines.

      No tracking

      No analytics and no usage counts. Nothing about how you use Cove is reported.

      Your own search

      File search uses Spotlight, which already runs on your Mac. Cove does not build a second index.

      Reports only when you send them

      Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on. Reports stay on your Mac until you choose to share one.

      Erase history

      Clear the list of recent apps in one step, from Settings, Privacy.
