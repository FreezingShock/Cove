Search

Search finds apps and files together.

Type once and the grid filters as you go. The best match gets a highlight, and matching files appear underneath. Search uses Spotlight, so anything Spotlight can find, Inlet can find.

  • Return opens the top hit
  • Apps and files in one list
  • Files come from Spotlight

      In practice

      Three things you will do every day.

      01

      Open an app you have not used in a week

      not
      NotesTop hit · Open
      ⌥Space→not→Return

      Notes is the top hit, and Return opens it. You never had to find it in the Dock or in Applications.

      02

      Find a file without remembering its folder

      budget
      Budget 2026.csvDocuments / Money
      ⌥Space→budget→Return

      Budget 2026.csv shows up from Spotlight, next to your apps. A file can be the top hit too, so Return opens it in its default app.

      03

      Start the day from the Dock

      Click the Inlet icon in the Dock

      A quick panel rises from the icon with your eight most recent apps and three recent files. The full grid is one button away.

      Built on what your Mac already has

      • Spotlight for files File search uses the index macOS already keeps. Inlet does not build a second one.
      • Your own app icons Each app shows the icon from its own bundle, so the grid looks like the Mac you already use.
      • SwiftUI and AppKit Built with the Mac's own interface frameworks, with no separate web layer.

      Motion

      Springs with fixed rules.

      Every animation runs on one of seven springs, each with a set duration and bounce. Things that open bounce a little. Things that close never do. With Reduce Motion on, everything becomes a short fade.

      • Open: 0.46 s, bounce 0.24
      • Press: 0.26 s, bounce 0.42
      • Dismiss: 0.26 s, no bounce

      Privacy

      Your data stays on your Mac.

      Inlet has no accounts and no analytics. It doesn't send your files, your searches, or your launch history anywhere. The one connection it makes on its own is a daily update check to GitHub, and you can turn that off.

      One update check

      Inlet checks GitHub about once a day for new versions. You can turn that off. It never sends your files, searches, or app list.

      No accounts

      There is no sign-in, no profile, and no sync between machines.

      No tracking

      No analytics and no usage counts. Nothing about how you use Inlet is reported.

      Your own search

      File search uses Spotlight, which already runs on your Mac. Inlet does not build a second index.

      Reports only when you send them

      Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on. Reports stay on your Mac until you choose to share one.

      Erase history

      Clear the list of recent apps in one step, from Settings, Privacy.
