      Start the day from the Dock

      Click the Inlet icon in the Dock

      By default the quick panel shows your eight most recent apps and three recent files. The full grid is one button away.

      Built on what your Mac already has.

      • Spotlight for files File search uses the index macOS already keeps. Inlet does not build a second one.
      • Your own app icons Each app shows the icon from its own bundle, so the grid looks like the Mac you already use.
      • SwiftUI and AppKit Built with SwiftUI and AppKit. There is no web layer.

      Your data stays on your Mac.

      Inlet has no accounts and no analytics. It doesn't send your files, your searches, or your launch history anywhere. The one connection it makes on its own is a daily update check to GitHub, and you can turn that off.

      One update check

      Inlet checks GitHub about once a day for new versions. You can turn that off. It never sends your files, searches, or app list.

      No accounts

      There is no sign-in, no profile, and no sync between machines.

      Your own search

      File search uses Spotlight, which already runs on your Mac. Inlet does not build a second index.

      Reports only when you send them

      Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on. Reports stay on your Mac until you choose to share one.

      Erase history

      Clear the list of recent apps in one step, from Settings, Privacy.
