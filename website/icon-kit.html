Inlet / Icon kit

Tiles, drawn straight.

52 icons built on the same 24px grid as the app icon. Sharp square strokes, one aqua square on each. Hover a tile to see it move.

24px gridEverything snaps to whole or half pixels.
1.5 strokeSquare caps and mitered joins. No rounded ends.
One accentA single filled aqua square, like the Grid icon.
Light and darkStrokes follow text color. Aqua stays aqua.
