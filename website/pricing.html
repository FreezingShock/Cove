What doesn't change on either plan.

Your data stays on your Mac

Searches, recent items and groups are saved on your Mac. Inlet does not upload your files or your app list.

The only network call is an update check

It goes to GitHub, and you can turn it off in Settings. Inlet has no analytics and no tracking.

Open source under MIT

Read it, change it and build it yourself. The license lets you do that for any purpose, free of charge.

Questions about the price.

What is the $5 for?

Inlet is built and maintained by one person. The $5 pays for that time, and in return you get the theme and zone tools, plus early builds.

Can I build it for free?

Yes. The MIT license allows it. A source build can turn on anything the code gates, so Supporter pays for the work and the early builds, not for locked features.

How do I pay?

Supporter opens with the 1.0 release. Until then, Inlet is free and there is nothing to buy.
