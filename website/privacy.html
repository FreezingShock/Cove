Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The short version. Inlet runs on your Mac and keeps its data there. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no ads. Inlet doesn't send your files, your searches, or your launch history anywhere. The one connection it makes on its own is an update check to GitHub, and you can turn that off. Crash reporting is off unless you turn it on, and even then reports stay on your Mac until you choose to share one.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers Inlet, the macOS app, and the Inlet website. It doesn't cover the other apps Inlet opens, which have their own policies. It also doesn't cover GitHub, which hosts Inlet's source code, release files, and public issues. GitHub's own privacy statement applies when you use it.

2. Data stored on your Mac

Inlet keeps its data in one folder: ~/Library/Application Support/Inlet. It doesn't store anything anywhere else.

3. Search stays on your Mac

Inlet searches apps and files through Spotlight, the search system built into macOS. Spotlight runs on your Mac. Inlet doesn't keep its own search index, and it doesn't save the text you type into the search field. Spotlight only finds what it has already indexed, so files in folders you have excluded from Spotlight won't appear in Inlet.

4. Network connections

Inlet's core features don't use the network. It makes two kinds of connection:

  • Update checks. Inlet uses the Sparkle framework to look for new versions. It checks once every 24 hours by default, and you can turn automatic checks off in Settings. You can also check by hand from the Inlet menu, Check for Updates. The check contacts github.com, which hosts Inlet's update feed and release downloads. GitHub sends downloads through its file storage host, objects.githubusercontent.com. Inlet doesn't contact any other server for updates. Each request includes the standard information a web request carries, such as your IP address, plus a user agent that names the Inlet version. Inlet won't install an update unless its digital signature checks out.
  • Crash reports and feedback. These only happen when you choose them. See section 5.

Visiting this website sends your browser's standard request to the web host. The host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, under its own privacy policy.

5. Crash reports and feedback

Crash reporting is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, Privacy, Inlet uses Apple's MetricKit to save a diagnostic report for each crash, hang, CPU exception, or disk-write exception. A report can include:

  • call stacks from the moment of the event, and the exception type
  • the reporting period, the Inlet version and build, and your macOS version
  • your Mac's hardware model

Call stacks can include file paths, and file paths can include your macOS user name. Inlet doesn't read your documents or your app list into a report.

Reports stay on your Mac, in Inlet's Diagnostics folder, up to 20 at a time. Nothing is uploaded automatically. A report leaves your Mac only when you share it yourself, in one of two ways:

  • Open GitHub Issue opens a prefilled issue on Inlet's GitHub page in your browser. You review it and submit it yourself. Anyone can read a GitHub issue.
  • Copy Full Report copies the report to your clipboard, so you can paste it wherever you like.

Send Feedback, in the Help menu, opens a small window in Inlet with a note field, a choice of reports to include, and a preview of everything that will be shared. The window doesn't send anything from inside Inlet. Its Open GitHub Issue button opens GitHub in your browser, and its Copy Full Report button copies the text.

6. Permissions

Inlet doesn't ask for Accessibility or Screen Recording access in version 1. You won't see those prompts. Launch at login uses the macOS login item setting, which you can change at any time in System Settings, General, Login Items.

7. Your choices

  • Erase launch history at any time from Settings, Privacy.
  • Turn automatic update checks off in Settings. You can still check by hand from the Inlet menu.
  • Keep crash reporting off, which is the default, or turn it on and off in Settings, Privacy. Erase saved reports from the same pane.
  • Turn off launch at login in System Settings, General, Login Items.
  • Delete Inlet and its data (section 8).

8. Data protection and deletion

Inlet's files sit in your user account, so they're protected by your Mac's account permissions. If you turn on FileVault, they're also encrypted on disk. To remove everything, quit Inlet, move it from Applications to the Trash, empty the Trash, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Inlet. Deleting that folder removes your settings, your launch history, and any saved crash reports.

9. Children

Inlet isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, because it doesn't collect personal information at all.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Changes that affect what data Inlet handles will also appear in the release notes on GitHub, before the update ships.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue on Inlet's GitHub issues page. Don't put personal information in a public issue. The support page covers how to report problems.
